All the light of the world, in high dynamic range.

Torches, braziers, lanterns, street lamps and candles each belong to a family with its own colour
temperature and brightness: a candle burns deep orange, a lantern warmer white, and a green lamp keeps
its own tint. Their light falls off as real light does, with no hard edge, and lands on the ground,
walls and characters with rough and glossy surfaces alike. Lanterns throw the shadow of their own
cage through baked cookies, lamps stay in their room, fires flicker, and the glass of a lamp glows.

The world is drawn in high dynamic range, so a bright pool fades to warm white instead of clipping,
and a faint lamp still reads in the dark. Where nothing is lit, the image is exactly the game's own.

The same lamps light the air: a field of their light that the fog multiplies by its own density, so
halos, beams and the shadows cut through them agree with the lit ground. Without the fog a thin halo
stands in for it. The sun, the moon and the sky are published too, so every effect shares one light.

Install it with wxl-graphics-extend (and DXVK for the lit surfaces). wxl-graphics-shadow adds every
shadow; wxl-graphics-fog adds the medium.
