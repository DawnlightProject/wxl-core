A fog that lives in the land. Cold fog forms on the ground at night, runs down the slopes in slow
rivers, fills the hollows until they brim and spill over the cols, streams along the valleys and
lies thick at the foot of cliffs. It reaches the horizon, drifts in banks with the wind, frays into
wisps as it passes in front of you, and burns off where the sun touches the ground.

Above it an atmosphere hangs in the whole air column: a smooth haze that swallows the distance,
dims the sky and holds every lamp's glow in the air, even where no fog lies.

It is a simulated medium, drawn by marching rays through it: sunlight and moonlight shade its
billows and pass through it in shafts between hills and trees, the sky lights it less in the
valleys, and every lamp, torch and fire glows in it through its own cage and glass. Inside
buildings the air is its own: still, dusty, lit by lamps, and the street's fog seeps in at the
door.

Bodies carve wakes through it, missiles punch tunnels that fill back with thick billows, chimneys
and campfires put smoke into it that rises and bends with the wind. Gameplay drives it through its
API: fog banks, fog walls that advance, fog that closes in on a point, gusts, pulses.

It runs as compute on the Vulkan device behind DXVK and needs Graphics Extend and Graphics Lights.
Without DXVK it stays inert and says why in its panel.
